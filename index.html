02 · why

Drugs don't evaporate. They leave the lungs inside microscopic droplets. We're building a ₹35 test that catches them at the roadside, in under a minute, with no lab and no network.

AeroSense

A handheld reader that catches the droplets in breath in a pre-cast hydrogel pad on a disposable electrode, reads them with a tiny potentiostat and classifies the result on the device. If someone won't blow, the same pad reads a fingertip instead.
